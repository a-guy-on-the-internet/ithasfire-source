Ithas Fire has three ways to find what you're looking for: browse, search, and filter.

## Browse

The [homepage](/) surfaces curated rails — trending events, events near you, and category-specific sections. Each card shows the title, venue, date, and starting ticket price.

## Search

Use the search bar at the top of any page, or go straight to [/search](/search). You can type:

- Event or artist name
- Venue name
- City or postcode
- Category keywords (music, comedy, community, etc.)

Results update as you type. Press **Enter** to open the full results page with filter controls.

## Filters

On [/search](/search), the controls let you narrow by:

- **Date** — pick a specific day or range
- **Price** — set minimum and maximum; sort low-to-high or high-to-low
- **Category** — filter by genre tags
- **Location** — enter a city, postcode, or toggle **Near me** to use your device's location
- **Distance** — radius from 5 km to 500 km, plus a **Worldwide** option

Sort options: **relevance**, **date**, **popularity**, **price**.

:::tip
Filter state lives in the URL — bookmark or share a filtered search and you'll land on exactly the same view every time.
:::

## Location permissions

When you toggle **Near me**, your browser asks for permission once. If you decline, you can still search by city or postcode manually.
