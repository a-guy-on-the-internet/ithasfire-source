An organiser can hand you a **group** of volunteers to run — a bar crew, a door team, a setup squad — as its **coordinator**. You manage just that group: reviewing who joins, checking people in, and pulling in extra hands. You don't need an admin role in the organisation, and you can't see or touch anything outside your group.

## Getting the invite

An organiser assigns you by email. If you already have an Ithas Fire account, you become a coordinator straight away. If you don't, you'll get an email inviting you to finish setting up an account with that address — once you do, you're coordinating.

Either way, the "you're coordinating…" email links straight to your **coordinator console** for that ask. Open that link to get started; bookmark it if you'll come back to it.

## What you can do

Your console shows only your group (or groups, if you run more than one for the same ask) — its members and their status. From there you can:

- **Review applications** — approve or reject people who applied to your group.
- **Check volunteers in** — mark them present when they arrive, so their hours are recorded.
- **Pull in helpers** — approved volunteers who aren't in any group yet sit in the ask's **general pool**. Use **Pull into {your group}** to bring one into your group, and release a member back to the pool if plans change.
- **Broadcast to your group** — send a one-way announcement (a meeting point, a time change, a thank-you) to everyone in your group by email and in-app notification.

If a volunteer has been promised a comp ticket, you'll see their reward status on the roster — **Comp granted**, **Comp used**, or **Comp voided** — but only the organiser's finance team can set up or change rewards.

## What you can't do

Coordinator access is deliberately narrow. You **can't** edit the ask itself, create or rename roles or groups, assign other coordinators, or act on volunteers in anyone else's group. Trying to reach another group's volunteers returns a "forbidden" message — that's the boundary working, not a bug.

:::tip
Pulling from the general pool only ever moves a volunteer **into your own** group. You can't move someone from one group to another — that's the organiser's call.
:::

## What can go wrong

- **"Forbidden" when opening the console** — you're signed in as someone other than the account the organiser invited. Sign in with the invited email address.
- **No pool to pull from** — every approved volunteer is already in a group, or none have been approved yet. Approve applicants to your group first, or ask the organiser to route more people your way.
- **You can't find the reward settings** — that's expected. Coordinators see comp status but never configure comps; that lives with the organiser's finance team.

For the organiser's side of this — creating the ask, groups, and assigning you — see [recruit standing volunteers](/help/organising-events/recruit-standing-volunteers).
