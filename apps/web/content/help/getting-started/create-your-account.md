Ithas Fire uses email + password, Google, or Apple for sign-in. All accounts land in the same place — you can buy tickets, manage orders, and (once Stripe is connected) sell tickets, all from one profile.

## Before you start

A valid email you can check right away — email-based sign-ups require clicking a verification link before you can complete a purchase.

## Option A — email and password

1. Go to the [sign-up page](/sign-up).
2. Enter your **name**, **email**, and a **password** (minimum 8 characters).
3. Click **Create account**.
4. Check your inbox for the verification email and click the link.

The verification page updates automatically the moment you confirm — you don't need to reload it.

:::tip
If the verification email doesn't arrive within a minute, check your spam folder and confirm the email you typed on the sign-up form.
:::

## Option B — Google or Apple

On the sign-up page, click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**. You'll be sent to the provider, authenticate there, then land back on Ithas Fire with your account already created and verified.

:::warning
Google and Apple sign-ups don't come with a password by default. To add one later, use [forgot password](/forgot-password) to set one — after that, you can sign in either way.
:::
