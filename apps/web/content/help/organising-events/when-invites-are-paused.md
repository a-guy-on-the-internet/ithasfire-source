When people mark your invite emails as spam, we stop sending invites for you until someone on our team has looked. This article covers why that happens, what keeps working while invites are paused, and how to get them back.

## Why invites get paused

Every personal invite you send from **Invited guests** goes out from our mail servers. When a guest presses "Report spam" in their mail app, their provider tells us. We count those reports for each organiser over the last 30 days, and pause invite emails when either of these is true:

- 3 or more people reported your invites as spam, or
- more than 1 in 100 of the people you invited reported them.

We count people, not emails. Someone who reports two of your invites counts once, and resending an invite to the same guest doesn't change the count.

The pause covers every event run from the same organisation or personal account, because spam reports affect the email address all of our invites come from.

Anyone who reports one of your invites as spam also stops getting invites from you, the same as if they had used the "Stop invites" link in the email.

## What you'll see

You get a notification saying your invites are paused. It says how many people reported your invites and links to the event they came from.

In the event's **Invited guests** panel, the form for adding a guest and the **Invite a contact list** button are replaced by a notice explaining the pause. **Resend** and **Invite again** are hidden on each guest.

## What still works

- Guests you already invited keep their links, and those links still let them in.
- **Copy link** still works on each guest, so you can send someone their link yourself.
- Your shared invite link still works. Share it anywhere you like.
- **Revoke** still works.

## Ask for a review

1. Open one of your gated events, go to its settings, and find **Invited guests** under **Access & privacy**.
2. In the notice, add anything we should know in the optional box. Say where the addresses came from, for example "These are people who bought tickets to our last two shows."
3. Press **Ask for a review**.

The notice changes to say when you asked. Someone on our team will look at it, and the answer shows in the same panel and as a notification.

:::tip
The most useful thing to tell us is how the people on your list asked to hear from you. Lists bought or copied from elsewhere draw the most spam reports.
:::

## After the review

- **We lift the pause.** You get a notification, and invites work again straight away. Spam reports about emails sent before the review no longer count against you.
- **We keep the pause.** The notice shows our note explaining why. Follow what it says, then press **Ask again** with any new detail.

## What can go wrong

- **You don't see the notice.** The **Invited guests** panel only appears on events gated by a password or an application. Open a gated event, or follow the link in the notification.
- **Sending says your invites are paused, but you didn't see the notice.** The pause started while you had the page open. The notice appears straight away; read it there.
