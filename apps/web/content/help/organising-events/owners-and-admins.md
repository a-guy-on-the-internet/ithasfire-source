Your group's **Team** page lists everyone in the group and their role. An Admin can invite people, change roles and remove members, but can't change or remove an Owner. Only an Owner can make someone else an Owner, by transferring ownership.

## Transfer ownership

Transferring makes another member an Owner and makes you an Admin, in one step. If anything goes wrong, nothing changes and you stay an Owner.

1. Open your group's admin area and go to **Team**.
2. Under **Transfer Ownership**, choose the member from **Select a member…** and click **Transfer**.
3. Read the confirmation and click **Confirm Transfer**.

When the transfer finishes you'll see **Ownership transferred**, and the Owner-only controls disappear from your Team page.

:::warning
You can't undo a transfer yourself. Once you are an Admin, only an Owner can make you an Owner again.
:::
