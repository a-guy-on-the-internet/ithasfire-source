You can refund your own tickets from your order page, without contacting anyone, while the order is still eligible. This article covers when that works, what you get back, and the cases where the button is missing.

## When a refund is available

Ithas Fire checks eligibility when you open the order. Every one of these has to be true:

- The event hasn't started.
- The organiser's refund deadline hasn't passed, if they set one. The deadline is shown on the refund page as **Refund Deadline**.
- The order was paid and hasn't already been fully refunded.
- The organiser hasn't marked the order non-refundable.
- There's no open card dispute on the order.
- The organiser's funds for the order haven't been paid out yet.

When a check fails, the refund page says which one in plain words, for example "The event has already started" or "This order has already been settled to the organizer."

Some tickets inside an eligible order can still be excluded:

- A ticket that was **scanned at the door** has been used.
- A ticket that is **listed for resale**. Cancel the listing first, then refund.
- A ticket you **gifted** to someone else. It belongs to the recipient now.
- A ticket whose revenue has already been **paid out** to the organiser.

## What comes back

The ticket price, the platform fee and any tax are refunded. A resale purchase has no platform fee, so its ticket price and tax come back. The payment processing fee is kept, because the card network doesn't return it to us. The exact amount appears on screen before you confirm.

A ticket a promo code or membership made free, or a ticket that cost $0, refunds $0: the ticket is returned and no money moves.

## How to request a refund

1. Open [/my-tickets](/my-tickets) and open the order.
2. For a single ticket, click **Refund** next to it. To refund everything, click **Request Full Refund** under "Need a full refund?".
3. Check the amount, then click **Confirm Refund**.

The money returns to the payment method you used. The order shows as **Refunded** when everything came back, or **Partially refunded** when some tickets stayed.

:::tip
Bought the ticket on the resale marketplace? Refund it as a whole order with **Request Full Refund**. The ticket goes back to the seller.
:::

## If the organiser cancels

Every ticket holder is refunded automatically. There is nothing to request; the refund appears in your account on the same timeline as any other refund, and the same rule applies: ticket price, tax and any platform fee come back, the payment processing fee does not.

A ticket that cost you nothing (made free by a promo code or membership, or priced at $0) is returned too, with nothing to refund.

A ticket someone gifted you is refunded to whoever paid for it, not to you.

If you paid cash at the door, the organiser gives it back; we can't refund cash.

## Processing time

Refunds appear on your statement within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

## If the check says no

If the page says you're not eligible but the circumstances warrant a refund (an accessibility problem, a duplicate order, an event that changed substantially), email [support@ithasfire.com](mailto:support@ithasfire.com) or the organiser. Organisers can refund tickets that the self-serve rules exclude, including ones already paid out to them.

:::warning
Contact support before filing a chargeback with your bank. A chargeback locks the order from any further refund while the dispute is open.
:::
