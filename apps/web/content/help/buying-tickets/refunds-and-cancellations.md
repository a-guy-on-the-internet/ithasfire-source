Ithas Fire checks refund eligibility automatically. The answer depends on the event's state and whether the organiser's funds have paid out.

## When a refund is available

Your request is eligible when **all** of these are true:

- The event hasn't started yet
- The order hasn't already been fully refunded
- The organiser's funds for this order haven't paid out yet

If any check fails, the UI tells you why with a reason code like `event_started`, `already_refunded`, `past_deadline`, or `paid_out`.

## Automatic refunds

When an **organiser cancels** an event, every buyer is refunded automatically — including the booking fee — within 5 business days.

## How to request a refund

1. Open [/my-tickets](/my-tickets).
2. Click into the order.
3. Click **Request refund**.
4. Confirm. The refund amount is shown before you submit.

Approved refunds return money to your original payment method. The order status changes to `Refunded` (full) or `Part refunded` (partial).

:::warning
Booking fees are non-refundable except when the event was cancelled by the organiser.
:::

## Processing time

- **Cards** — 5–10 business days to appear on your statement
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay** — usually same day, up to 5 business days
- **ACH / bank account** — up to 10 business days

## If the automatic check says no

If eligibility denies you but you believe circumstances warrant a refund (accessibility issue, duplicate order, event substantially changed), email [support@ithasfire.com](mailto:support@ithasfire.com). Support can sometimes intervene manually.

:::tip
Contact support before filing a chargeback with your bank. We resolve most issues faster directly, and a chargeback locks the order from further action while the dispute is open.
:::
