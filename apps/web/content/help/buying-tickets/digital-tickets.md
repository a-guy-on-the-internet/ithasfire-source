Every Ithas Fire ticket is a QR code. Bring it on your phone, in your wallet app, or printed on paper — any of them work.

## Where to find your tickets

- **Web app**: [/my-tickets](/my-tickets) — always the source of truth
- **Mobile app**: open **Tickets**, then tap an order to see its passes and what you paid
- **Email**: your confirmation email has a direct link
- **Wallet**: click **Add to Apple Wallet** or **Add to Google Wallet** on the order detail page
- **Paper**: click **Print tickets** on the order detail page for a printable sheet (see [Paper backup](#paper-backup))

## At the door

Venue staff scan the QR code.

In the mobile app, tap a ticket to open its pass. The app turns your screen brightness up on its own while the pass is showing — scanners read reflected light, and a dimmed screen is the most common reason a code takes a few tries. Brightness goes back to normal when you leave the pass.

If your order has several tickets, use the arrows under the code to move between them. Each one is a separate QR.

:::tip
Every ticket has its own QR code. You can keep multiple tickets on one device and scan everyone in together, or [transfer](/help/buying-tickets/manage-your-orders) one to each guest so the ticket lives in their own account.
:::

## No signal at the venue

Basements, packed rooms, and thick walls all eat phone signal, so the app does not rely on having it.

Once you have opened an order in the app, its passes are saved to your device. If you are offline at the door, the pass still opens and still scans — it just shows an **Offline copy** label with the time it was saved.

:::tip
Open your order once before you set off, while you still have signal. That is what saves the passes for the night.
:::

Two things to know about the saved copy:

- It shows the ticket's status as of when it was saved, so a ticket scanned by someone else in the meantime may still look unused until you are back online.
- Saved passes are cleared when you sign out, and are removed a few days after the event.

## If a pass says the ticket has moved

The pass screen shows **This ticket has moved** when that specific ticket is no longer on the order — normally because it was transferred to someone else or refunded. Your remaining tickets are unaffected; tap **Back to order** to see them.

## Paper backup

Open your order at [/my-tickets](/my-tickets) and click **Print tickets** in the **Your tickets** section. That opens a printable sheet — three tickets to a page, each with its QR code, the event details, and a tear-off stub carrying the order reference and a **Door initial** box for staff to sign if scanning fails.

From there use your browser's own print dialog. Choosing **Save as PDF** keeps a copy you can email or print later. You do **not** need to turn on "Background graphics" — the QR codes print either way.

:::tip
One sheet covers the whole order, so a group of four prints once and cuts along the dashed lines. Only tickets that are still valid appear — anything transferred, refunded, or already scanned is left off, and the sheet stamps the date and time it was printed so door staff can spot an out-of-date printout.
:::

The printed QR scans exactly like the one on your phone. Note that paper does not update: if you transfer or refund a ticket after printing it, the printed copy stops working immediately.

## Transferred tickets

When you transfer a ticket, **the original QR is invalidated at the moment of transfer**. Only the recipient's new code works at the door.

:::warning
Never buy tickets from third-party resellers outside Ithas Fire. QR codes only scan once, and scammers commonly sell screenshots of tickets they've already used. Resale isn't currently available on Ithas Fire — if someone you trust can't use their ticket, ask them to send it with **Transfer**, which is the only tracked way to hand a ticket over.
:::

## Rejected at the door?

If your QR scans as already-used, it usually means either someone scanned it before you, or the ticket was transferred to another account. Contact the organiser via the event page's **Contact organiser** link — they have tools to re-issue tickets.
