All your purchases live at [/my-tickets](/my-tickets) on the web. Tabs split them into **Upcoming** and **Past**. In the mobile app, open **Tickets** or **Profile** to see ticket codes and statuses grouped by order.

:::tip
Your next night out sits at the top of the account menu on the web: the soonest event you hold a ticket for, have RSVP'd to, or are on the lineup for. For tickets, **Show my tickets** opens that order (or your ticket list, if the tickets were transferred to you). For an RSVP or a lineup spot, **Open event** goes to the event page. When nothing is coming up, the menu shows no block at all.
:::

## Verify your email first

If you haven't verified your email, the tickets section of [/my-tickets](/my-tickets) and the **Tickets** tab in the app ask you to first. Click **Email me a code** and enter the code we send you. Tickets bought with your address, on an organiser's website or given to you, then appear in your account. This keeps someone who signs up with your address from seeing your tickets.

:::tip
The tickets in your inbox work at the door whether or not you verify.
:::

## Order detail

Click any order to open its detail page. You'll see:

- Event name, date, and place
- Status badge — `Confirmed`, `Payment processing`, `Partial Refund`, `Refunded`, `Pending`, `Cancelled`, or `Disputed`
- Each ticket with its QR code
- Actions: **Add to Apple Wallet**, **Add to Google Wallet**, **Transfer**, **Request refund**, **Resell** (not currently available)

## Add to a wallet

Click **Add to Apple Wallet** or **Add to Google Wallet**. Ithas Fire generates the pass and your phone's wallet app opens to import it. Once imported, the ticket lives in your wallet and updates automatically if the event changes.

:::tip
Open your wallet app before you arrive. Cellular signal and Wi-Fi get flaky when crowds queue up.
:::

## Transfer a ticket

Click **Transfer** on a ticket. Search the recipient by email, confirm, and Ithas Fire sends them the ticket as a new order in their own account. **The original QR is invalidated at the moment of transfer** — only the new owner can check in.

If the recipient doesn't have an Ithas Fire account yet, they get an email invite to claim the ticket. The invite lasts 7 days, or until the event ends if that comes sooner.

Transfers close when the event ends. From then on the **Transfer** button is replaced by a note saying so, and an invite that hasn't been accepted yet can no longer be claimed.

While a refund on the ticket is being processed, including one from the event being cancelled, you can't transfer it, send an invite for it, or accept an invite for it. Try again in a few minutes.

## Request a refund

Click **Request refund** on the order. Ithas Fire checks eligibility automatically (event hasn't started, not already refunded, organiser hasn't paid out yet). If eligible, the refund is processed back to your original payment method.

## Resell a ticket

Resale is not currently available on Ithas Fire — if you can't use a ticket, use **Transfer** instead. When resale returns and the organiser enables it, a **Resell** action appears on each eligible ticket. See [resale marketplace](/help/buying-tickets/resale-marketplace) for how the flow works — eligibility, fees, and the post-event hold on seller payouts.

:::warning
**Transfer** only appears on a ticket that is still valid and hasn't been transferred, refunded, or paid out to the organiser, and it goes away once the event ends. Resale is not currently available. If you don't see a button, that action isn't available for that ticket.
:::
