Some events — usually house shows and private venues — don't show their exact address publicly. Instead of a pin, the map shows a shaded circle around the general area, or no map at all with just a neighbourhood or city name.

That's deliberate: the organiser has obscured the location, and the exact address unlocks for **ticket holders only** at a set reveal time.

## Before the reveal

The event page shows the obscured view plus a notice like _"Exact address unlocks {date} for ticket holders."_ That date is the reveal time the organiser chose — anywhere from the moment of purchase to a custom date, with "24 hours before start" being typical.

If you buy before the reveal, your ticket confirmation email shows the same locked notice instead of a street address. Your ticket itself is completely normal — see [digital tickets](/help/buying-tickets/digital-tickets).

## At the reveal time

Once the reveal time passes, every ticket holder gets:

- **An in-app notification** — "Location revealed: {event}" with the full address. This always arrives; you can review it any time at [/account/notifications](/account/notifications).
- **An email** — "The location for {event} is now live" — with the address, a map link, and a directions link.

The exact address also appears on the event page itself from that moment on.

:::tip
Buying after the reveal time? You skip the wait — the exact address is in your ticket confirmation email straight away.
:::

## The address isn't showing for me

- **The reveal time hasn't passed yet** — check the date in the notice on the event page.
- **You're not signed in** — the event page only shows the exact address to signed-in ticket holders. Sign in with the account you bought with.
- **You don't hold a ticket** — the exact address is never shown publicly, even after the reveal time. Saving or following an event isn't enough.
- **The email is missing** — check spam, and check the in-app notification at [/account/notifications](/account/notifications), which is always delivered. Your [notification settings](/help/account/notification-settings) control the other channels.

:::warning
Never share a revealed address publicly — these events obscure their location to protect the host's home or a private venue. Bring your ticket; the address alone won't get you in.
:::
