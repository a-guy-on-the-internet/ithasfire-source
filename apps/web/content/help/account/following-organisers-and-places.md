Follow places, artists and organisers to receive alerts about their new public events.

## Follow a page

Sign in, open a place, artist or organiser page, and click **Follow**. Click the button again to unfollow. Following does not subscribe you to past events.

## Manage your follows

Open [Settings → Notifications](/account/settings/notifications). The **Following** section lists the pages you follow and links back to each page.

- Turn **New event alerts** on or off for each page.
- Turn **Only nearby events** on to limit alerts by distance. Enter a whole number from 1 to 500 km; the default is 100 km.
- Click **Save preferences** to apply your changes, or **Unfollow** to remove a page.

The nearby filter uses the event location from your latest paid or completed order with a usable location. If no such location is available, nearby alerts are not sent. Turn the nearby filter off to receive alerts without a distance limit.

## Choose delivery channels

Under **Organizer updates**, below your Following list, choose whether to receive alerts in the app, by email or by push notification. Push delivery requires a registered device. Following alerts do not use SMS.

Alerts cover newly published public events associated with a page you follow. They do not announce private events or reveal hidden place locations.

## Visibility

Private pages and pages with following disabled cannot be followed. You cannot follow your own personal page.

Page owners see aggregate follower statistics, not a list of followers' names. Public follower counts appear only when the page enables them.
