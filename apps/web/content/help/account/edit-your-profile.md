Your account settings live at [/account/settings](/account/settings). Each area — Profile, Email, Security, Notifications, and so on — is its own page, listed in the settings navigation (a sidebar on a wide screen, a tappable list on a phone).

## 01 — Profile

Editable fields:

- **Name** — shown to organisers on your orders
- **Avatar** — click your picture, pick an image, then frame it in the **Crop your photo** window (see below)
- **Job roles** — what you do (Musician, DJ, Comedian, …). Your first two roles appear as chips at the top of your **public page**; if you pick **Other**, the description you type is what shows.
- **Genre tags** — music/event genres you care about. These also appear as chips on your public page — unless your page has its own genre tags section, which takes over when it has any tags.

Changes save when you click **Save**.

:::tip
Job roles and genre tags are public: anyone viewing your page sees them. Remove them here (or curate your page's own genre tags section) if you'd rather not show them.
:::

### Cropping your photo

Picking an image no longer uploads it straight away. A **Crop your photo** window opens first so you choose exactly which square shows up as your avatar:

1. Drag the image to reposition it, and use the zoom slider (or the **+** / **−** buttons) to decide how close in you sit.
2. Click **Recentre** to go back to the starting frame at any time.
3. Click **Apply crop** to upload, or **Cancel** to pick a different image.

Nothing is uploaded until you click **Apply crop** — cancelling leaves your current avatar untouched. Only the visible square is uploaded, so a large phone photo becomes a small, sharp avatar rather than a slow-loading original.

:::tip
The crop window works without a mouse. Tab to the image and use the arrow keys to move it, **+** and **−** to zoom, and **Home** to recentre — or use the on-screen arrow, zoom and **Recentre** buttons, which do the same thing. Hold **Shift** with an arrow key for one-pixel nudges.
:::

The same crop step runs on your **public page** editor, where the avatar is cropped square and the banner to a wide 3:1 strip. One exception there: an animated GIF is uploaded exactly as you picked it, because cropping would freeze it to a single frame.

## 02 — Email

1. Enter the new address.
2. Click **Send verification code**.
3. Open the new inbox and copy the 6-digit code.
4. Enter the code in settings and click **Verify code**.

:::warning
Your account keeps the old email until you verify the code. Check your inbox before closing the tab.
:::

:::tip
If you signed up with Google or Apple and never set a password, you normally can't change your email from inside settings because the address is provided by the identity provider. If Ithas Fire cannot deliver email to that address, settings will ask you for a reachable address so receipts, tickets, and security codes can reach you again.
:::

## 03 — Notifications

Per-category switches for each channel — in-app, email, push, and SMS. Order confirmations and receipts are always delivered in-app, and SMS unlocks once you verify a phone number. Full details in [notification settings](/help/account/notification-settings).

:::tip
If your email address hard-bounces or reports spam, Ithas Fire may pause email until you verify a reachable address — so keep [your email settings](/account/settings/email) up to date.
:::
