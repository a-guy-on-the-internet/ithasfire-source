Control what Ithas Fire tells you about — and on which channels — from [Settings → Notifications](/account/settings/notifications).

## Categories and channels

Notifications are grouped into five categories, each with its own switch per channel (**In-app**, **Email**, **Push**, and **SMS** where texts are offered):

| Category                            | What's in it                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Order confirmations & receipts**  | Purchase confirmations, e-tickets, refund notices, and payout updates            |
| **Event announcements**             | Line-up changes, schedule updates, and important event news from organisers      |
| **Event reminders**                 | Day-before reminders for events you RSVP'd to                                    |
| **Promotions & recommendations**    | Early-bird deals, personalised picks, and featured events near you               |
| **Organizer updates**               | News and updates from organisers you follow                                      |

Each switch saves as soon as you flip it. In-app and email start switched on.

## Text messages

When Ithas Fire can send texts, an **SMS** switch appears for **Order confirmations & receipts** only. If you don't see it, texts aren't offered right now. Texting is optional and starts switched off. Receipts and tickets never go by text: the one text in this category is the [address reveal](/help/buying-tickets/where-is-this-event) for an event you're going to.

The SMS switch stays locked until you verify a phone number. We text US numbers only (+1). **ADD PHONE NUMBER** takes you to [Settings → Security](/account/settings/security), where **SEND CODE** texts you a 6-digit code. Tapping it is your consent to security-code texts; turning on the SMS switch is your consent to notification texts.

Reply **STOP** to any Ithas Fire text and every text to that number stops, volunteer reminders included. To get texts again, reply **START**, or switch SMS off and on again. Reply **HELP** for help. Msg & data rates may apply. See the [SMS terms](/terms#sms).

Volunteer reminders stay off after a STOP until you tick the text-reminder box on that sign-up again, on its row in [My volunteering](/my-volunteering) or on the event page. If the sign-up's number is your verified phone, that turns texts back on; for any other number, also reply **START** from it. A sign-up that isn't listed in your account has no box, so its reminders stay off.

### Order confirmations & receipts are always on in-app

The in-app switch for **Order confirmations & receipts** is locked on ("Always on — required for purchases"). These are the messages that deliver your tickets, receipts, and refund notices — the in-app copy is always delivered, so there's always a record at [/account/notifications](/account/notifications) even if an email goes astray. This category also covers the [address reveal](/help/buying-tickets/where-is-this-event) for hidden-location events.

## Notification history

Every in-app notification you've received lives at [/account/notifications](/account/notifications) — the **History** button at the bottom of the Notifications section takes you there.

## What can go wrong

- **The SMS switch is greyed out**: you haven't verified a phone number yet.
- **"We can only text US numbers."** when you tap **SEND CODE**: the number isn't a US +1 number. Canadian and other non-US numbers can't get texts.
- **A toggle won't save** — you'll see a "Couldn't save preferences" message and the switch rolls back. Try again in a moment.
