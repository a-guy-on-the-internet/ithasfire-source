Control what Ithas Fire tells you about — and on which channels — from [Settings → Notifications](/account/settings/notifications).

## Categories and channels

Notifications are grouped into four categories, each with its own switch per channel (**In-app**, **Email**, **Push**, **SMS**):

| Category                            | What's in it                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Order confirmations & receipts**  | Purchase confirmations, e-tickets, refund notices, and payout updates            |
| **Event announcements**             | Line-up changes, schedule updates, and important event news from organisers      |
| **Promotions & recommendations**    | Early-bird deals, personalised picks, and featured events near you               |
| **Organizer updates**               | News and updates from organisers you follow                                      |

Each switch saves as soon as you flip it. In-app and email start switched on; **SMS always starts switched off** — texts are strictly opt-in.

### Order confirmations & receipts are always on in-app

The in-app switch for **Order confirmations & receipts** is locked on ("Always on — required for purchases"). These are the messages that deliver your tickets, receipts, and refund notices — the in-app copy is always delivered, so there's always a record at [/account/notifications](/account/notifications) even if an email goes astray. This category also covers the [address reveal](/help/buying-tickets/where-is-this-event) for hidden-location events.

## Setting up SMS

SMS switches stay disabled until a **verified phone number** is on file. If you haven't added one, the Notifications section shows "Add a phone number to receive SMS updates" with an **ADD PHONE NUMBER** button that takes you to the phone section of the same settings page.

To verify a number:

1. Enter your phone number, including the country code.
2. Click **SEND CODE** — Ithas Fire texts you a 6-digit code. Sending the code is also where you consent to receive SMS from Ithas Fire.
3. Enter the code to confirm the number is yours.

Once verified, the SMS switches unlock immediately and you can opt in per category. A verified number can also receive two-factor authentication codes by SMS if your authenticator is unavailable.

:::tip
Reply **STOP** to any Ithas Fire text to opt out of SMS entirely — this is honoured at the carrier level, independent of your toggles here. Reply **HELP** for help. Message and data rates may apply.
:::

Replying **STOP** also records the opt-out on your Ithas Fire account, so every SMS to that number stops — including volunteer shift reminders — even if a toggle here still shows on. Replying **START** (or re-enabling an SMS switch on this page) turns notification texts back on, but it does **not** re-subscribe volunteer reminders: opt back into those from the volunteer settings of the specific event.

## Notification history

Every in-app notification you've received lives at [/account/notifications](/account/notifications) — the **History** button at the bottom of the Notifications section takes you there.

## What can go wrong

- **A toggle won't save** — you'll see a "Couldn't save preferences" message and the switch rolls back. Try again in a moment.
- **SMS switches are greyed out** — you don't have a verified phone number yet. Add and verify one first.
- **You expected a text but didn't get one** — check that the SMS switch for that category is on, and that you haven't replied STOP from that number.
