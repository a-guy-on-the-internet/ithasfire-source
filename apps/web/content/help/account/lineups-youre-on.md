An organiser can add your Ithas Fire profile to an event's lineup without asking you first. When they do, you're told, and you can take yourself off it if you shouldn't be there.

## When you're told

You get a notice called **You're on the lineup for** followed by the event's name. It says who added you and in what role (Performer, DJ, Host, Volunteer, and so on). You're told in the app — in your notifications, and as a push notification if you use the Ithas Fire app — and by email when your email address is verified.

- If the event is already published, you hear straight away.
- If it's still a draft, you hear when the organiser publishes it.
- If you're taken off and added back to the same event within a week, you aren't told a second time. The exception is when you took yourself off: if an organiser adds you back after that, you're always told.

This notice is always sent, whatever your [notification settings](/help/account/notification-settings) say, so you always know where your name appears.

If an organiser invited you by email to set up payouts, that invite is the email you get. The lineup notice itself is in the app.

## See your lineups

Open your account menu and choose **My lineups** (the row appears only when performer mode is on, under Account settings → performer section; without it, go straight to `/my-lineups`), or follow **Review your lineups** in the notice. Only you can see this list. If a lineup is your next night out, it also shows at the top of the account menu as **You're playing**, with **Open event** to go to its page.

If you haven't verified your email, **My lineups** asks you to first: click **Email me a code** and enter the code we send you, and your lineups appear.

Each event shows its date and time (in the event's own time zone), your role, and the organiser (the organisation or person running the event). Upcoming events come first, then past ones. Some events carry a label:

- **Not public yet** — the event is a draft. You can see it here, but it has no public page yet.
- **Cancelled**, **Archived** or **Ended** — the event won't happen, or already has.

Published events have a **View event** link to their public page.

## Take yourself off a lineup

1. On **My lineups**, find the event and select **Remove me**.
2. Read the confirmation, then select **Remove me** again. Select **Keep me on** if you change your mind.

The event disappears from your list and your name comes off its lineup. The organiser is told you took yourself off: the owners, admins and editors of the organisation running the event, or the person running it. Nobody is told when there's no one else to tell:

- on a community listing that no organiser runs,
- on your own event,
- on an organisation's event where you're its only owner, admin or editor.

The confirmation says whether anyone will be told.

:::warning
If you're on the lineup as a **Volunteer** and signed up to volunteer at that event, removing yourself withdraws your volunteer signup too. The confirmation tells you when this applies.
:::

:::warning
If the show was booked through a [request to play](/help/account/performer-mode-and-booking-requests) that named you, removing yourself from an upcoming show also gives up your spot on that booking. This is final: you can't rejoin that booking, even if the organiser adds you back to the lineup. The confirmation tells you when this applies. If the show has already started, or has been cancelled or archived, removing yourself only takes your name off the lineup.
:::

The organiser can add you again later. If they do, the event comes back to **My lineups**.
