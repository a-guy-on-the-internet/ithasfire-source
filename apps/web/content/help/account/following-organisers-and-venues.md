Follow the venues, artists, and organisers you care about to hear first when they announce new events.

## How to follow

On any venue, artist, or organiser page, click **Follow** next to the name. You can unfollow anytime from the same button.

## What you get

- **New event notifications** — email and/or push when someone you follow publishes an event
- **Nearby filter** — optionally restrict notifications to events within a distance you choose (uses the area around your recent orders)

Change the channels and the distance filter under [Settings → Notifications](/account/settings/notifications).

## Limits and visibility

- You can't follow your own venue or organiser page
- Private pages can't be followed
- Rate limit: 20 follow / unfollow actions per minute

## What entity owners see

Organisers and venue owners see **aggregate** follower stats only — totals and recent growth. They never see who follows them by name.

Follower counts on a page are hidden by default. A venue has to opt in before showing "N followers" publicly.

:::tip
Follow venues in a city you visit often and you'll get a steady trickle of the right events instead of trawling search every week.
:::
