## Market Size

- **Global online event ticketing:** $50-85B (2025)
- **US online ticketing:** ~$12.5B
- **US concert/event promotion:** ~$60B
- **Independent live sector (NIVA 2025):** $153.1B total economic output, $86.2B direct GDP, 153,000+ events, 183.7M fans, 1,500 NIVA member venues
- **64% of independent stages were unprofitable in 2024**
- **Secondary ticket market:** StubHub S-1 shows $8.68B GMS, $1.77B revenue (2024), resale markups avg 49%, up to 712% in uncapped states

## Serviceable Addressable Market

- Indie venues under 2,000 cap
- Clubs ≤750 cap: avg 278 tickets at $34.74, grossing ~$10K/show
- 751-1,500 cap: avg 769 tickets at $33.7K/show
- **1% of ~150,000 indie US events/year at 8% take on $25 avg ticket = $7.5M annual gross revenue**

## Regulatory Tailwinds

- **FTC Junk Fees Rule:** Effective May 12, 2025, all-in pricing mandatory, penalties up to $51,744/violation
- **Live Nation antitrust verdict:** April 15, 2026, jury found illegal monopoly. DOJ term sheet: 15% fee cap, 13 amphitheater divestitures, open-ticketing requirements
- **State resale reform:** Maine/Maryland caps, California AB 1720, New York face-value bill, UK banned resale above face value

## Competitive White Space

No single competitor combines:
1. Per-artist fan attribution with data export
2. Anonymous performer venue reviews (payout, sound, booker)
3. Artist-controlled resale with fees back to artist
4. Built-in venue discovery/booking for artists

## Historical Graveyard

- **Songkick/CrowdSurge:** $61M burned, LN employees prosecuted for hacking, $110M settlement, shut down
- **TicketFly:** 1,100 venues, $500M GMV, acquired by Pandora $450M, sold to Eventbrite $200M, killed by 27M-user data breach
- **Jukely:** ClassPass-for-concerts, unit economics never worked
- **Rukkus:** Thin-margin aggregator, sold to TickPick for undisclosed

## Comparable Bootstrap Success

- **Ticket Tailor:** 14 years to $6.6M ARR, 34 employees, 73,000 creators, 25M tickets/year, B Corp, bootstrapped, UK-native

## Viability Assessment

**12-month:**
- 10-15% strong outcome (5-25 organizers, $2K-$20K MRR)
- 50-60% median (0-5 paying organizers, still iterating)
- 30-40% stalling

**36-month:**
- 5-8% Ticket Tailor trajectory ($500K-$2M ARR)
- 15-20% small-business ($50K-$500K ARR)
- 60-70% dead or walking dead

**Venture-scale (<3% probability). Acquisition ≥$5M (~5-8%).**

## Key Insight

The "Robin Hood of the music industry" vision is validated — structural whitespace exists in fan attribution + venue intelligence that no competitor has built. Bootstrap trajectory (Ticket Tailor model, decade-long build) is realistic. The window is open due to LN antitrust verdict and Eventbrite acquisition by Bending Spoons.
